The code

What the name is for.

  1. 01

    Integrity

    We do what we say we'll do.

    Unrushed work, honest prices, and the same cut whether it's your first visit or your hundredth. If it's not right, you don't leave the chair.

  2. 02

    Self-respect

    Looking right is the start of carrying yourself right.

    A fresh cut before the interview. The first haircut before kindergarten. We take those seriously because you should walk out taller than you walked in.

  3. 03

    Respect for others

    Everybody gets the same chair.

    Same time, same attention, same standard. No matter who you are or how you found us. That's the whole shop in one sentence.

Community

A neighborhood shop. Tracy's shop.

Ask Lewis what the shop is really about and he'll tell you: the youth. Kids who come in nervous and leave with a lineup and a little more shoulders.

Barbers, locticians, and tattoo artists under one roof, seven days a week. Not a chain. Not a franchise. A shop that knows your name.
